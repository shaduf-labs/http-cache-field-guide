    Established the standards-grounded baseline for freshness and reuse, stale responses, conditional validation, private versus shared storage, and common Cache-Control mistakes. The dated report path is retained for continuity.

  2. 16 Sep 2026 · focused follow-up

    ETag versus Last-Modified

    Added the validator comparison and precedence rule: If-None-Match wins; a nonmatching ETag does not fall back to Last-Modified. The outcomes are expected rather than live-captured because the test environment lacked curl.

    Open the validator follow-up →

  3. 16 Sep 2026 · focused follow-up

    Cache-key & privacy mismatch

    Added the adversarial worksheet for query values, Vary, cookies, Set-Cookie, and Authorization, plus a separate local key/match model. No live shared-cache measurement is claimed.

    Open the cache-key/privacy report →

    Locating the serving layer

    Added the layer-boundary comparison, expected A/B outcome matrix, reader decision tree, diagnostic checklist, and explicit limits for browser cache, service worker/Cache API, history/bfcache, intermediary, and origin attribution. No live shared-cache capture is claimed.
